About Me

Hello! I am Chengran Li (李程然), an undergraduate student in cybersecurity at Wuhan University.

My research focuses on AI security and privacy. I am particularly interested in how knowledge can move safely and effectively across models, devices, and organizations. My recent work includes one-shot personalized federated graph learning, model-merging security, defenses against prompt injection in AI agents, multimodal copyright protection, and proactive defenses against generative-AI misuse.

Propagation-Aware Collaborative Calibration for One-Shot Personalized Federated Graph Learning

Privacy · Federated Learning · Graph Neural Networks

PACE combines propagation-aware low-rank model transfer with receiver-side convex calibration. With a single communication round, each client can selectively adopt cross-community knowledge, fall back exactly to its local model under conflict, and reduce one-way communication to 10%–17% of a dense model.

AI Security · Model Merging · Safety Alignment
